Built for the coast

Wind and salt are what wear a Fylde roof out

Coastal roofs take more wind than most. Slates and ridge tiles get lifted, mortar weathers faster in salt air, and flashing around chimneys is often where the first leak starts. Most of the leaks we see begin small.

  • Ridges, verges and chimney flashing checked on every visit
  • Photos of everything we find, so you see what we see
  • Gutters checked so water actually leaves the roof
  • A written quote before any work starts
