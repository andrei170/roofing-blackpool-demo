Roofing on a coast is not roofing inland

It is the wind and the salt that decide how long a roof lasts here.

Wind lift, not just rain

On the Fylde coast the common failure is wind getting under an edge or a ridge, not water soaking through the middle. That changes where you look and what you fix.

Fixings corrode faster

Salt air shortens the life of nails, clips and flashings. A repair using the same fixings that just failed is a repair you will pay for twice.

Gutters matter more

Driven rain plus a blocked gutter puts water against the fascia and the roof edge, which is how a gutter job becomes a roof job.
